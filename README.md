# Battery Guard

A lightweight Android battery-threshold alarm for the **Hiby M300** DAP (Android 13).

It runs quietly in the background and shows a **full-screen alarm** (like a clock
alarm) when the battery:

- drops to your **minimum** → *"charge me"* (default 30%)
- rises to your **maximum** while charging → *"unplug me"* (default 80%)

Designed for **battery longevity** and **minimal energy use** (event-driven, no
polling). Setup is two sliders. Bilingual (English / Spanish). Dismiss the alarm
by touching or swiping the screen.

See [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) for the full design and build/install steps.
